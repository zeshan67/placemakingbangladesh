About Us

A collective movement: not owned, not authored, but carried forward by the people who shape space through daily life.

Placemaking Bangladesh lives in conversations, gatherings, memories, and gestures that turn the ordinary into meaningful place.

If you’ve ever made a space feel more welcoming, more alive, and more yours, you’re already part of it.

We, the team behind this platform, are simply facilitators. Our role is to connect, document, support, and amplify the placemaking that is already happening across Bangladesh to the world. We work across research, design, storytelling, and community engagement, but always with the belief that real placemaking comes from the ground up.

Placemaking Bangladesh is not about us. It’s about you.

We’re just here to help carry the movement forward, until you carry it further.
